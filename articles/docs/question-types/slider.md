Add a question, choose Slider, and set its range and step.

## Settings

- **Minimum, maximum and step.** The range and how finely the respondent
  can place the handle.

## Best practices for research

Label both ends with what they mean. A slider that starts at a value
records it as an answer, so check what was moved.

Reference: Dillman, D. A., Smyth, J. D., & Christian, L. M. (2014).
*Internet, phone, mail, and mixed-mode surveys: The tailored design
method* (4th ed.). Wiley.
