## Settings

- **Number of stars.** The top of the scale. The answer is the number of
  stars given.

## Best practices for research

Use stars for an overall judgement. For a scale of 2 or more items, use
Likert questions that share a choice set.

Reference: Dillman, D. A., Smyth, J. D., & Christian, L. M. (2014).
*Internet, phone, mail, and mixed-mode surveys: The tailored design
method* (4th ed.). Wiley.
