## Settings

- **Required.** Asks for an answer before the respondent goes on. Leave
  open questions optional where you can.

## Best practices for research

Use a short text where the possible answers are too varied to list.
Where the answers can be listed, a choice question is quicker to answer
and to analyse.

Reference: Dillman, D. A., Smyth, J. D., & Christian, L. M. (2014).
*Internet, phone, mail, and mixed-mode surveys: The tailored design
method* (4th ed.). Wiley.
