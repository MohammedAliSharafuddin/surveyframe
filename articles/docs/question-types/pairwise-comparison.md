# Add pairwise comparisons

[Documentation](https://surveyframe.org/articles/docs.md) / [Question
types](https://surveyframe.org/articles/docs.html#question-types)

Choose the task first. To find which criteria matter most (AHP, ANP),
use Saaty’s importance scale. To find how criteria influence each other
(DEMATEL), use the influence scale. Then add a question, choose Pairwise
comparison and list the criteria.

## Settings

- **Criteria.** List the things compared. On Saaty’s scale each pair is
  asked once, since judging A against B also answers B against A. On the
  influence scale each pair is asked in both directions, since the
  influence of A on B can differ from that of B on A.
- **Scale.** Choose Saaty’s 1 to 9 for which matters more and by how
  much (AHP, ANP), or 0 to 4 for how much 1 influences another
  (DEMATEL).

## Best practices for research

Keep to 7 criteria or fewer: the pairs grow fast, and the consistency
ratio shows when judgements contradict each other.

Reference: Saaty, T. L. (1980). *The analytic hierarchy process*.
McGraw-Hill.
