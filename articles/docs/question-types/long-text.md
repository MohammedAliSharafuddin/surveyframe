## Settings

- **Required.** Leave it off for an open comment, so a respondent with
  nothing to add moves on and the comments you receive are considered
  ones.

## Best practices for research

Ask 1 specific thing, such as what to change. Specific prompts give
answers you can code, where a request for any comments gives answers
that are hard to compare.

Reference: Krosnick, J. A., & Presser, S. (2010). Question and
questionnaire design. In P. V. Marsden & J. D. Wright (Eds.), *Handbook
of survey research* (2nd ed., pp. 263-313). Emerald.
