## Settings

- **Choice set.** Add the options respondents put in order. Each option
  gets its own column in the data, holding its position, 1 for first.

## Best practices for research

Rank 5 options or fewer: beyond that, the middle of a ranking says
little.

Reference: Krosnick, J. A., & Presser, S. (2010). Question and
questionnaire design. In P. V. Marsden & J. D. Wright (Eds.), *Handbook
of survey research* (2nd ed., pp. 263-313). Emerald.
