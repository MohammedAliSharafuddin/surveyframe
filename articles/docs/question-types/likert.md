## Questions, response options and scales

A **question** asks 1 thing and is stored as 1 column. Its **response
options** are the labelled choices it offers, such as Strongly disagree
to Strongly agree, and related questions share 1 set of options. A
**scored scale** combines a group of related questions into 1 score, and
its reliability is checked as a whole.

To build a scored scale:

1.  Add 1 Likert question for each item of the scale, each with 1 idea.
2.  Give every item the same choice set.
3.  In Scale, assign each item to the scale it scores into.
4.  Tick Reverse-scored for each item worded against the others.
5.  In the analysis plan, declare the scale’s reliability and the
    analyses that use its score.

## Settings

- **Choice set.** The answer options, the labelled points of the scale
  such as Strongly disagree to Strongly agree. The items of 1 scale
  share 1 choice set.
- **Scale.** The scored scale the item belongs to, so its items combine
  into 1 score and their reliability is checked together.
- **Reverse-scored.** For an item worded against the others, so a high
  answer means the same on every item.

## Best practices for research

Label every point of the scale, and keep each item to 1 idea, so every
respondent answers the same question.

Reference: Krosnick, J. A., & Presser, S. (2010). Question and
questionnaire design. In P. V. Marsden & J. D. Wright (Eds.), *Handbook
of survey research* (2nd ed., pp. 263-313). Emerald.
