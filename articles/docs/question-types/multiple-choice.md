## Settings

- **Choice set.** Add the answer options. Each becomes its own column in
  the data, ticked or left blank.
- **Required.** Tick it to require at least 1 answer.

## Best practices for research

Prefer asking yes or no for each option when every option matters: a
choose-any list makes an unticked box ambiguous.

Reference: Dillman, D. A., Smyth, J. D., & Christian, L. M. (2014).
*Internet, phone, mail, and mixed-mode surveys: The tailored design
method* (4th ed.). Wiley.
