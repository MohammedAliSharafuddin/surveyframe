# Articles

## Questionnaire templates

10 example questionnaires, each with an analysis plan to adapt and
screenshots of the study in SurveyStudio, grouped by field.

- [Questionnaire
  templates](https://surveyframe.org/articles/templates.md):

  10 example questionnaires, each with an analysis plan to adapt, in
  psychology, education, business, events and tourism, and research
  methods.

- [Course evaluation
  survey](https://surveyframe.org/articles/templates/course-evaluation-survey.md):

  A course evaluation in which students rate each part of a course on 1
  grid, compared between students who took it in person and online.
  surveyframe gives you the questionnaire, the analysis plan and a
  report for the teaching team.

- [Customer satisfaction
  survey](https://surveyframe.org/articles/templates/customer-satisfaction-survey.md):

  A customer study of return intention: whether customers who use a
  service more come back, and how that use relates to their satisfaction
  and the offer they prefer. surveyframe gives you the questionnaire, a
  plan with logistic regressions declared in advance, and the report.

- [Employee engagement
  survey](https://surveyframe.org/articles/templates/employee-engagement-survey.md):

  An engagement survey of 8 items written to measure 2 dimensions, how
  work is organised and the content of the work, with a check that
  employees’ answers support 2 scores. surveyframe gives you the
  questionnaire, the analysis plan and the report.

- [Likert scale
  questionnaire](https://surveyframe.org/articles/templates/likert-scale-questionnaire.md):

  A short questionnaire that measures 1 idea with a 4-item Likert scale,
  including a reverse-worded item, and checks that the scale holds
  together before its score is used. surveyframe gives you the
  questionnaire, a pre-analysis plan to submit with your proposal, and
  an APA 7 report.

- [Measurement model questionnaire (CFA and
  SEM)](https://surveyframe.org/articles/templates/measurement-model-questionnaire.md):

  A study exploring a proposed relationship between content quality,
  satisfaction and the intention to return. Specify the measurement and
  structural model, then generate syntax for lavaan or seminr.
  surveyframe gives you the questionnaire, the model syntax and the
  report.

- [Open-ended feedback
  survey](https://surveyframe.org/articles/templates/open-ended-feedback-survey.md):

  A feedback survey of what worked and what to change, analysed as text:
  frequent words and phrases, words in context, sentiment and topics.
  surveyframe gives you the questionnaire, the analysis plan and the
  report.

- [Pilot survey with a small
  sample](https://surveyframe.org/articles/templates/pilot-survey-small-sample.md):

  A pilot with 18 participants that answers the main study’s questions
  with methods suited to a small sample: a rank-based comparison, an
  exact test and a penalised logistic regression. surveyframe gives you
  the questionnaire, the pre-analysis plan and the report.

- [Preparing a survey study for
  preregistration](https://surveyframe.org/articles/templates/pre-registered-survey-sealed-and-amended.md):

  A survey study whose instrument and analysis plan are saved with a
  hash before data collection, with any later change recorded as a
  disclosed amendment. surveyframe writes a pre-analysis plan for you to
  complete and register on OSF or Zenodo, and a report that names the
  instrument it analysed.

- [Supplier and venue selection (decision
  making)](https://surveyframe.org/articles/templates/supplier-and-venue-selection.md):

  Compare candidate suppliers or venues using the criteria your
  committee values. Record the criteria and the judgements, then review
  the rankings your selected methods produce. surveyframe gives you the
  questionnaire, the analysis plan and the report.

- [Training evaluation before and
  after](https://surveyframe.org/articles/templates/training-evaluation-before-and-after.md):

  Compare the same participants’ confidence, and their willingness to
  recommend a workshop, before and after the training. Plan the paired
  comparisons before collecting responses. surveyframe gives you the
  questionnaire, the plan and the report.

and recording the study, each in SurveyStudio and in R.

- [How to analyse a grid of rating

  logic](https://surveyframe.org/articles/guides/combine-1-question-at-a-time-with-skip-logic.md):

  Compare an outcome across 3 or more groups, add a second factor, and

- [How to compare 3 or more measurements of the same

  Find which measures go together with an outcome, and how much of it

- [How to predict a yes or no, an ordered rating or a

- [How to prove a questionnaire file is the one you
  planned](https://surveyframe.org/articles/guides/prove-a-questionnaire-file-is-the-one-you-planned.md):

- [How to show a survey 1 question at a

- [How to test a measurement and structural
  model](https://surveyframe.org/articles/guides/test-a-measurement-and-structural-model.md):

  Declare the measurement and structural model for your constructs, take

- [How to test mediation and
  moderation](https://surveyframe.org/articles/guides/test-mediation-and-moderation.md):

  Test whether a variable carries an effect (mediation) or changes its

- [How to test whether 2 categorical answers are
  associated](https://surveyframe.org/articles/guides/test-whether-2-categorical-answers-are-associated.md):

  Test whether 2 categorical answers are associated.

- [How to weight a survey sample to its
  population](https://surveyframe.org/articles/guides/weight-a-survey-sample-to-its-population.md):

  Apply survey weights so your summaries describe the population the
  sample was drawn from.
