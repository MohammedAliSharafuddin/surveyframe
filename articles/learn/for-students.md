# Survey research for your studies

This page is for a student conducting a survey for a course, a
dissertation or a thesis. It takes you from an example study to your own
report. Your supervisor and ethics committee may ask for the documents
named at each step.

## Your steps

1.  **Set up.** Install R, RStudio and surveyframe as the [Get
    started](https://surveyframe.org/articles/start.md) page shows.
2.  **Choose an example close to your study.** The [questionnaire
    templates](https://surveyframe.org/articles/templates.md) are
    grouped by field. Each holds a questionnaire and an example analysis
    plan to adapt.
3.  **Adapt the questionnaire.** Open the example in SurveyStudio, edit
    the questions on the Design screen and press **Save to Studio**.
    [Question
    types](https://surveyframe.org/articles/learn/question-types.md)
    explains which question fits each kind of answer.
4.  **Write your analysis plan.** On the Plan screen, record each
    research question, what you expect to find, the measurements and the
    method. [The analysis
    plan](https://surveyframe.org/articles/learn/analysis-plan.md)
    explains each part.
5.  **Share the plan before collecting.** On the Report screen, press
    **Export pre-analysis plan** and give it to your supervisor or
    ethics committee with the `.sframe` file.
6.  **Collect and check responses.** The Collect screen gives you the
    survey web page. Load the responses on the Responses screen and
    review them on the Quality screen.
7.  **Analyse and report.** Run the planned analyses on the Analyse
    screen, write your interpretation and export the APA 7 report. If
    you changed the plan, the report lists each amendment with its
    reason.
