# Survey research for NGOs and research organisations

This page is for an NGO or research organisation planning its own survey
research. It keeps the questionnaire, the analysis decisions and the
report for stakeholders in 1 connected record.

## Your steps

1.  **Set up.** Install R, RStudio and surveyframe as the [Get
    started](https://surveyframe.org/articles/start.md) page shows.
2.  **Start from an example.** The [questionnaire
    templates](https://surveyframe.org/articles/templates.md) include
    customer satisfaction, employee engagement and decision-making
    studies to adapt.
3.  **Prepare the pre-analysis plan.** Record each research question,
    the measurements and the method on the Plan screen, then export the
    plan from the Report screen. [The analysis
    plan](https://surveyframe.org/articles/learn/analysis-plan.md)
    explains each part.
4.  **Collect responses your way.** The Collect screen gives you a
    survey web page to host, and an optional collector that stores each
    response in a Google Sheet your organisation owns.
5.  **Document decisions.** A change to the plan after collection starts
    is recorded as an amendment with its reason.
6.  **Report to stakeholders.** Export the APA 7 report as HTML, Word,
    PDF or OpenDocument. It presents the planned analyses, the results,
    your interpretation and the recorded amendments.
